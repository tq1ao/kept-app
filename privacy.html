Privacy Policy

Kept · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version: Kept keeps what you save on your iPhone. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your notes, and we don't collect any data about you.

What Kept stores, and where

Everything you save — people, notes, dates, gift ideas, photos you add — is stored only on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us or anyone else. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), your Kept data is included in that backup, which Apple or your computer looks after under its own terms. You can export everything as text, or delete everything, from Settings in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Kept has no account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no advertising. We don't know who uses Kept or what they save.

Features that use your phone's permissions

  • Contacts — Kept uses iOS's own contact picker. Only the one contact you pick is shared with Kept, and only on your phone. Kept never asks for access to all your contacts.
  • Calendar — if you choose to, Kept reads your calendar to import dates, and can write your dates to a "Kept" calendar on your phone. This stays between Kept and your calendar.
  • Notifications — reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification passes through a server.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — when you speak a note, it's turned into text on your phone. The recording isn't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos — photos you add to a note are copied into Kept on your phone.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Kept uses Apple's on-device model to understand your notes, answer questions and suggest ideas. This runs on your phone; your notes aren't sent to us or used to train anything.

When Kept uses the internet

  • Links and price checks — when you save a link to a gift, or turn on price checks, your phone opens that shop's page directly to read its name, picture and price. The shop sees an ordinary visit from your phone, as it would in a browser. Nothing goes through us.
  • Opening links — links open in Safari, under the website's own privacy policy.
  • Kept Pro — purchases are made and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether Kept Pro is active.

Children

Kept isn't directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
