Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no server. Keep your iPhone backed up (iCloud or a computer) so your notes come with you to a new phone. You can also export everything from Settings → Export.

How do I restore Kept Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings in Kept and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Kept, Settings → Kept Pro → Manage subscription — or on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps everything you've saved; only Pro's extras stop at the end of the period.

Why didn't a reminder arrive?

Check that notifications are on for Kept (iPhone Settings → Notifications → Kept), then open Kept once so it can set the next reminders.
